💰 Master Loot

WoW-style loot for Owlbear Rodeo — with letters, books and journals your players will actually want to read.

Install

  1. In Owlbear Rodeo, open Extensions → Add Extension.
  2. Paste this manifest URL:
  3. Enable Master Loot in your room.

How it works

  • GM: right-click any token → Loot to fill it with items, letters, books or journals, then flip on Lootable.
  • A glowing coin bag appears on the token.
  • Players: click the bag to open the loot window; click a written item to read it on immersive, aged paper.
  • Readers can adjust text size and zoom — the settings are remembered in their browser.
